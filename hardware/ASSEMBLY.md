# Luxor — assembly

Before you start: coupons A, B and C passed, `check_fit.py` is green, the battery header polarity is confirmed on the board, and firmware v9 is flashed and working on USB **with the battery unplugged**.

You need: parts 1–3, the board, the battery, 3 × M2×6 screws (or the length `luxor_params.scad` prints), a 0.3 mm foam or rubber gasket ring (cut from gasket tape), Kapton tape, a 22 mm quick-release strap with spring bars, a small Phillips/hex driver, and CA glue or 0.1 mm double-sided tape for the inlay.

1. **Clean the prints.** Remove the brim. Run a 1.1 mm drill by hand through each spring-bar hole. Check that each flexure tab moves freely; clear any strings in its U-slot with a blade.
2. **Inlay (two-tone only).** Dry-fit the gold inlay in the shell's front pocket, with the double index tick at 12 o'clock (by the key notch). Then fix it with three tiny dots of CA glue on the pocket floor, or a ring of 0.1 mm tape. Press it flat for a minute.
3. **Gasket.** Lay the 0.3 mm gasket ring on the inside of the lip, around the opening.
4. **Board into the shell.** USB-C at 6 o'clock, aligned with the mouth. Lower the board glass-first into the shell until the glass rests on the gasket. Press each button: you should feel the switch click through the tab.
5. **Battery.** Insulate the board's back with one layer of Kapton where the cell will sit. Plug the cell in, having checked the polarity with a multimeter. Lay it in the cover's cradle, with the lead running along the floor channel towards 6 o'clock. Do not fold the lead over the cell.
6. **Close.** Fit the cover with its 12 o'clock key into the shell's notch. Start all three M2 screws by hand, then tighten them in turn, a little at a time, until the cover meets the shell rim. **Stop at first resistance**: the screws go into the brass standoffs, and over-tightening strips them or loads the glass.
7. **Strap.** Fit the quick-release spring bars into the lug holes.
8. **First power-up on battery** — off the wrist, on a non-flammable surface. Check that Settings → About shows a sensible battery voltage, the case does not get warm, and the watch runs for 10 minutes. Then charge it on USB while you watch it.

Opening it again: remove the three screws and lift the cover; the board comes out of the shell front-first.
